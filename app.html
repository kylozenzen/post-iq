About Ben

PostIQ was built by Ben Campbell, a social media and content strategist who wanted a calmer way to see a Buffer queue, plan around it, and notice gaps before they happened.

It started as a set of internal workflow tools and became a public beta companion for Buffer users.

Plan a campaign or newsletter

A dated plan across a range — a newsletter issue or a campaign. Tick off the stages as you go. This is a local plan; it never schedules a Buffer post.

Stages

    Alerts

    Channel health, your Buffer connection, and any channel running low on content in the next couple of days.

    Posting schedule

    Tell PostIQ when you plan to post on each channel. The calendar uses this to show your days ahead, meaning how many days you're covered. These are local planning choices; your Buffer posts never change.

    Connect Buffer and sync to set a schedule.

    Posting days

    Plan time off

    Taking a break? Mark dates off so a planned vacation doesn't show as a gap on your calendar. Your Buffer posts stay exactly as scheduled.

    Want to keep posting while you're away? For every reshare-enabled channel, PostIQ can queue your kept posts (from Past posts on the Shelf) into Buffer on each empty posting day in this range, at that channel’s usual time. Each kept post goes out once per channel, so your streak stays alive without repeats.
