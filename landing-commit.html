Plan

A calendar with room in the margins.

Buffer's calendar shows your posts. PostIQ adds the planning around them: the reminder to reshare, the holiday to work around, the idea you want to come back to on Friday.

  • Notes on any day. Plans, holidays, ideas, and rest days, color-coded and draggable.
  • Day Brief in the composer. Start a post and that day's notes are right there.
  • Repeats and awareness days. Monthly recaps and a ready-made pack of observances.
  • Your Google Calendar alongside. See launches and time off next to your posting plan.
  • Gaps by channel. Spot the open Threads slot even when LinkedIn is full.

Health

Know when something didn't go out.

Posts fail quietly. A connection expires, a channel gets paused, and you find out a day later. PostIQ checks your channels and tells you in plain words what happened and how to fix it.

  • Channel status at a glance. Disconnected, paused, and needs-reconnect channels.
  • Failed posts with the reason. Buffer's own error message and the help article for it.
  • Retry in one tap. Put a failed post back in the queue, or duplicate and reschedule it.
  • Comments to answer. Sent posts show their comment count and link straight to the post.

Runway

Know when you can take a day off.

When your queue is full, PostIQ says so and tells you to rest. As a channel gets close to empty, it nudges you to write something new or repurpose something that already worked.

  • Days covered per channel. Counted against your real posting schedule, skipping paused days.
  • Gentle nudges. "You're set for two weeks" now, "Threads runs out Thursday" later.
  • Pace check. How far ahead you write, how often you post after 10 PM, and your longest stretch without a break.

Questions

Before you connect.

Does PostIQ replace Buffer?

No. Buffer still schedules and publishes. PostIQ reads your Buffer account and adds planning, health checks, and runway on top.

Where do my notes live?

Dated ideas sync to your Buffer account, so they follow you between devices.

Will it post anything without asking?

No. Retry and repurpose only run when you tap them.

What does it cost?

PostIQ is free during the public beta. You need a Buffer account to connect.
