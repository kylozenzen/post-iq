# PostIQ Launch Copy

## 1. Short product blurb

PostIQ is a free public beta companion workspace for Buffer users. Plan your queue, compose posts, split threads, build content pillars, and share clean content snapshots before publishing.

## 2. Founder story blurb

I built PostIQ because I needed a cleaner workflow for planning, writing, and sharing social content. When Buffer opened up its API, I connected the tools to Buffer and turned them into a public beta for other creators and social teams.

## 3. Buffer Discord post draft

## 4. LinkedIn launch post draft

Social media managers do not need another place to be busy.

They need a cleaner way to move from “what are we posting?” to “this is ready to go.”

That was the itch behind PostIQ.

I originally built it for myself because my social workflow had too many tabs, too many half-finished ideas, and too much context living in my head. I wanted one practical workspace for planning the queue, writing posts, splitting threads, organizing content pillars, and sharing a clean plan before publishing.

Then Buffer opened up its API, which made it possible to connect that workflow to the tool many creators and teams already use to publish.

So I turned the internal toolkit into PostIQ: a free public beta companion app for Buffer users.

It currently helps with:

- Calendar planning
- Compose workflows
- Thread splitting
- Ideas and Content Pillars
- Templates / snippets
- Snapshot links for no-login content review
- Sending content through Buffer when connected

It is intentionally not trying to replace Buffer. It is the planning layer before Buffer.

If you manage social content, work with approvals, or just want fewer “where did that idea go?” moments, I’d love for you to try it and send feedback.

Public beta: `https://postiq.netlify.app`

Smart workflows beat heroic tab juggling. Usually.

## 5. Snapshot-specific post draft

A tiny PostIQ feature I’m excited about: Snapshot.

It turns a content plan into a clean shareable link — no login required for the person reviewing it.

Useful for sending a week or month of planned content to a teammate, client, or stakeholder without screenshots, spreadsheet gymnastics, or “can you request access?” chaos.

Snapshot links are static copies, so anyone with the link can view the included content. Simple, practical, and intentionally boring in the best way.

Try it in the PostIQ public beta: `https://postiq.netlify.app`
