What PostIQ adds

One calendar for the queue and the thinking around it.

Buffer stays where you publish. PostIQ is the place to look at what’s coming, decide what’s missing, and plan before you send anything.

01

Scheduled posts, with context

See confirmed Buffer posts in month, week or agenda view, alongside your planning notes and to-do actions for the same day.

02

Gaps based on your rhythm

Pick posting days and a target for each channel. PostIQ marks the days that rhythm expects and your queue doesn’t cover.

03

Saved content, ready to date

Keep material in Content Bank and give an item a target date. It shows on the calendar as a plan, clearly marked not scheduled.

04

Coverage before time away

Check a period, mark intentional days off or a lighter stretch, and see which planned slots still need a post before you step away.

Queue Health & Your Pace

Know what’s covered, slot by slot.

Queue Health shows each channel’s confirmed runway. Your Pace checks any period of up to 92 days against your rhythm and lists every uncovered slot.

  • Per-channel posting days and posts-per-day targets
  • Intentional days off and lighter periods, without touching Buffer posts
  • “Plan this slot” opens that date on the calendar
Coverage requires Buffer sign-in and a recent sync.
Content Bank

Turn saved material into a dated plan.

Search what you’ve saved, find items without a date, and plan a local target. Buffer scheduling is shown separately, so a plan is never mistaken for a post.

  • Filter by no target date, local target, or confirmed Buffer scheduling
  • Develop channel versions while the original source stays intact
  • Templates, Content Pillars and Trending live under Resources
Saved locally in your browser; works in the preview.

Your Buffer connection

You decide every Buffer action.

PostIQ uses Buffer’s public API with OAuth (PKCE). When you sign in, it can:

  • Read your channels, scheduled queue and recent posts
  • Create a draft, queue a post or schedule one only when you choose that action
  • Leave existing Buffer posts alone when you change plans, rhythms or days off
  • Disconnect from PostIQ settings, or revoke access in Buffer, at any time
