How PostIQ works

  • No login: PostIQ does not require a PostIQ account.
  • Token handling: your Buffer token is stored in sessionStorage or localStorage, based on your choice.
  • Thread Splitter: split long copy into editable thread parts and send as draft/queue/scheduled.
  • Content Calendar: view scheduled posts, then add color-tagged planning notes per day.
  • Media workflow: If your post needs images or video, save it as a draft in PostIQ, then open Buffer to attach media and schedule or publish it there.
