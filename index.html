The story

Built for me.
Opened for everyone.

When Buffer launched its API beta, I got early access. I was already deep into managing social — growing large audiences for brands and organizations — and the API opened up possibilities the UI just didn't expose.

So I built PostIQ for myself. A planning calendar that showed my whole month at a glance. A composer that sent posts straight to my Buffer queue without switching tabs. An approval flow so clients could review content before it went live — without needing access to my account.

It made my workflow noticeably cleaner. So I cleaned it up, opened it to the community, and kept building.

Trust & setup

Your Buffer token —
plain and simple.

PostIQ uses your Buffer API token to read your schedule and send drafts when you ask it to. Here's exactly how it works.

01

What it is

A private code from Buffer that connects PostIQ to your account. Get it from buffer.com/developers in under a minute.

02

What PostIQ does with it

Reads your channels and scheduled posts. Sends drafts, queues posts, or schedules them — only when you explicitly click a button.

03

Where it lives

In your browser only — session storage or local storage, your choice. PostIQ never stores or transmits your token to any server.

Only if you share it. Treat it like a password — don't paste it somewhere public. You can revoke it anytime from Buffer settings.
