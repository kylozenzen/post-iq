Common questions

Quick answers to obvious questions.

You'll get the most from PostIQ with Buffer connected — sync channels, see your queue, and publish. You can explore the app before connecting, but Buffer is required to send posts.
PostIQ adds the planning layer that comes before the queue: content pillars to guide what you write, a calendar view to spot gaps, AI-assisted starters to beat blank-page paralysis, a thread splitter for long-form content, and a snapshot and approval flow for teams. Buffer handles scheduling and publishing — PostIQ handles everything that happens before you hit send.
PostIQ only reads your channels and queue, and creates content when you explicitly choose a Buffer action — Draft, Queue, or Schedule. It uses Buffer's official OAuth, the same flow you'd use with any trusted Buffer integration. You can revoke access from Buffer's own account settings at any time, independently of PostIQ.
No. Nothing publishes unless you explicitly choose a Buffer action — Draft, Queue, or Schedule. PostIQ never touches your accounts without your direct instruction.
Under five minutes. Connect your Buffer account via OAuth and PostIQ pulls in your channels automatically. From there you can start composing, queueing, or scheduling straight away — no setup calls, no onboarding forms.
PostIQ is free during public beta. Some features may evolve as Buffer's API matures. The core workflow — plan, compose, split, snapshot, approve, and publish — is stable and ready to use.
No. PostIQ is independently built by Ben Campbell, a social media strategist. It is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Buffer unless otherwise stated.
