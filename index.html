PostIQ — How It Works

Welcome to PostIQ

PostIQ connects to your Buffer account and adds tools Buffer hasn't built yet. Setup takes about 2 minutes.

  • Step 1 — Sign in with Google or email
  • Step 2 — Go to publish.buffer.com/app-password and create an access token
  • Step 3 — Paste it into the Connect screen — PostIQ saves it to your account
  • Step 4 — Hit Load from Buffer — your channels and posts populate automatically

You only do this once. PostIQ remembers your connection after that.

What's free?

Everything. PostIQ is free during beta. No credit card, no trial. Use Send Feedback in the sidebar to report issues or request features.

Thread Splitter Hero Tool

Paste any long-form content and get a structured thread — hook, body parts, CTA. Sends as a native thread to Twitter/X or Threads via Buffer.

  • Paste rough notes, a blog section, or a long LinkedIn draft
  • Hit Split into thread — PostIQ structures it automatically
  • Edit any part in the preview panel, add or remove parts
  • Pick your platform, then queue or schedule to Buffer

Tips

  • Twitter/X parts target 240 chars — edit any that run long
  • Threads parts can go up to ~500 chars
  • The first part is always your hook — make it punchy

Content Calendar

Your scheduled Buffer posts on a monthly grid with color-coded planning notes layered on top.

  • Colored dots on dates = scheduled Buffer posts by channel
  • Note dots = your planning notes (Gold = Idea, Blue = Draft, Green = Campaign, Red = Urgent)
  • Click any date to see scheduled posts and add notes
  • The Today strip shows today's posts and notes at a glance
  • Type in the Today strip and hit Enter to drop a quick note instantly

Post Composer New

A lightweight composer with rich text that actually renders on LinkedIn, Threads, and more.

  • Select text → hit B or I for bold/italic
  • Or type **bold** / *italic* then hit Aᵤ to convert to Unicode
  • Use the bullet tool for bullet points on any line
  • LinkedIn link field adds a preview card attachment
  • Pick a channel, then Add to queue or Schedule for later

Evergreen Library

Your sent post history. Best for text-based posts worth resurfacing.

  • Loads your last 100 sent posts from Buffer on connect
  • Browse and filter by channel
  • Click any post to edit and re-queue
  • Best for text posts — threads, LinkedIn updates, tweets

Beta Notes

PostIQ is built on Buffer's public GraphQL API beta. A few honest constraints:

  • Analytics data isn't available via the API yet
  • Image/video upload coming soon (Unsplash integration in progress)
  • Instagram Reels thumbnails not exposed in Buffer's API
  • PostIQ is independent — not affiliated with or endorsed by Buffer
