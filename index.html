Trust and setup

What the Buffer key does —
and what it does not.

Your Buffer API key is a private access code from Buffer that lets PostIQ connect to your account. It gives PostIQ permission to read parts of your workflow and send actions back to Buffer when you choose to save a draft, queue a post, or schedule content. Think of it like a private permission pass for your Buffer account.
01

What it actually is

Your API key is a private code generated by Buffer. PostIQ uses it to securely connect to your Buffer account so the app can work on your behalf inside your own workflow.

02

What it lets PostIQ do

With your permission, PostIQ can read parts of your Buffer setup and send actions back when you choose to save a draft, queue a post, or schedule content. It does not post on its own.

03

Where it lives and how you control it

Your key stays in your browser on your device. You can save it just for the session or keep it locally for convenience, and you can revoke or replace it anytime from Buffer settings.
