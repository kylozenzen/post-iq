How your Buffer key works —
quick and clear.

Your Buffer API key is a private token from Buffer. It lets PostIQ connect to your account and act only when you choose to draft, queue, or schedule.
01

What it actually is

Your API key is a private code from Buffer that securely connects PostIQ to your account.

02

What it lets PostIQ do

It can read key account details and send actions to Buffer when you click save, queue, or schedule. It never posts on its own.

03

Where it lives and how you control it

Your key stays in your browser. Save it for the session or locally, and revoke or replace it anytime in Buffer settings.
