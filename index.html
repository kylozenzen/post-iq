Stop letting your best
posts die once. Buffer Power Tools for serious social teams.

PostIQ connects to your Buffer account and surfaces your top evergreen posts, splits long content into native threads, gives you a content calendar with planning notes, and helps you post smarter — not more.

♻️
Post Recycler
Pulls your last 100 sent posts directly from Buffer via the API. Browse them by channel or date, pick any post worth reposting, edit the copy, then send it back to your Buffer queue or schedule it for a specific time — without leaving PostIQ.
Uses: posts · channels · createPost via Buffer GraphQL API
🧵
Thread Splitter
Paste any long-form content — a blog intro, a LinkedIn post, a brain dump — and PostIQ breaks it into a properly structured thread with a hook, body parts, and a CTA. Sends it to Buffer as a native thread (Twitter/X or Threads) using Buffer's thread metadata support.
Uses: channels · createPost (thread metadata) via Buffer GraphQL API
🔒
Your account, your data
Sign in with Google or email. Your Buffer access token is saved securely to your PostIQ account so you never re-enter it. PostIQ only reads and writes to Buffer on your behalf — your posts never touch our servers directly.
Auth via Firebase · Buffer app password token
📅
Content Calendar
See all your upcoming scheduled Buffer posts laid out on a monthly grid. Click any date to add color-coded planning notes — Idea, Draft, Urgent, Research, or Campaign — and queue any note directly to Buffer with one click.
Uses: posts (scheduled) · createPost · Firestore notes
⚠️
Beta Software — Expect Rough Edges
PostIQ is built on top of Buffer's public GraphQL API beta, which Buffer is still actively developing. Some features may behave unexpectedly, API endpoints can change without notice, and capabilities are limited to what Buffer currently exposes publicly. We ship fixes fast — use the feedback button inside the app to report anything broken.
