PostIQ

Public beta · no login required

A lightweight Buffer companion for faster planning, drafting, and scheduling workflows.

Use PostIQ to organize ideas, split threads, view scheduled content, and prep posts before sending them into Buffer for final scheduling, queuing, or media attachment.

🧵 Thread Splitter

Paste long-form content and split it into thread-sized parts you can copy, draft, queue, or schedule in Buffer.

📅 Content Calendar

View scheduled Buffer posts in a monthly grid and keep color-tagged planning notes by day.

✍️ Post Composer

Draft text-first posts with formatting tools. For posts that need media, save as a draft and finish in Buffer.

🔗 Read-only month sharing

Generate a shareable planning snapshot link for the current month, with optional notes.

How PostIQ works

  • No login: PostIQ does not require a PostIQ account.
  • Token handling: your Buffer token is stored in sessionStorage or localStorage, based on your choice.
  • Thread Splitter: split long copy into editable thread parts and send as draft/queue/scheduled.
  • Content Calendar: view scheduled posts, then add color-tagged planning notes per day.
  • Media workflow: If your post needs images or video, save it as a draft in PostIQ, then open Buffer to attach media and schedule or publish it there.
